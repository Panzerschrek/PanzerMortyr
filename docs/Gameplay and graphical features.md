This document lists various gameplay and graphical features of the original game.
All of them should be implemented in PanzerMortyr.

* Enemies (drawn as models).
* Static objects, sometimes destructible (like barrels).
* Static pickups (placed during map creation).
* Dynamic pickups (dropped from enemies).
* Planes - they are like enemies, but just fly in circles and can't be killed.
* Light-related sprites (coronas, flames).
* Projectiles - from some player and enemy weapons.
* Projectile smoke traces including flamethrower flames.
* Shot bullet visual effects - a fast-traveling line representing a bullet is drawn, even if the actual bullet travels instantly.
* Bullet holes (drawn as decals).
* Footsteps - decals drawn on some surfaces.
* Blood marks (drawn as decals). They seem to be used also for some enemies (like officers).
* Smoke effects drawn at bullet hit point shortly after hit.
* Sparks drawn sometimes at bullet hit point shortly after hit.
* Flame effects drawn around exploded barrels some time after explosion happened.
* Flames burning at flamethrower hit points, sometimes on enemies or even the player.
* Debris produced by some destructible objects (like explosive barrels).
* Rats.
* Crows (they may be seen on map 1).
* Muzzle flash light sources - both for player and enemies.
* Flash light sources at bullet hit point.
* Flashlight - as a dynamic light source created at point of intersection of the view vector with map geometry.
* Night vision - all is drawn greenish and dark places receive a little bit more light.
* Infrared vision - all is drawn redish and enemies/pickups are glowing bright.
* Breakable windows - with splinters falling down and sky drawn where the broken window was (not always).
* Planar reflections on some surfaces (like floors).
* Water surfaces (with alpha-blending and maybe even some animation).
* Glass (with alpha-blending).
* Underwater caustics.
* Sun or Moon.
* Fog volumes.
* Global fog.
* Snowflakes.
* Rain, including water circles on some surfaces (like water, but not only water).
* Doors, including onces operated by a button requiring a key.
* Platforms (lifts), including onces operated by a button (or even two buttons at top and at bottom).
* Water where player can swim.
* Map switch triggers.
* Secret triggers (one can be seen on map 18).
* Player speech at some scripted map locations.
* Items displaying texts (books, notes), mostly used for storytelling.
* Alarms - with flashing red light and maybe even a corresponding sound. They seem to be destructible.
* Ambient sound sources.
* Enemy sounds - speech, shots, death sounds.
* Player sounds - footsteps, jump sounds, weapon sounds, pickup sounds, death sounds.
